    Shipping Policy

    As a valued member of the AdV family, providing cost effective and reliable shipping is a top priority for us. The below shipping charges are designed to offer a uniform shipping cost to both Canada and the US. Shipping amount is a percentage of the total product value, including back orders.

    Please contact customer support or your sales representative should you have any questions.

    Effective November 1st, 2026

    General Info

    This freight policy is applicable to all orders that are not included in promotional or pre-booking programs. Specific terms and conditions may apply. Please read below.

    Shipping Rates

    Canada

    Order amountFreight charges
    Under $3,000No cap / Full freight cost
    $3,000 and overFree freight

    Actual freight charges will apply for delivery to high-freight areas or remote locations (Canadian Territories, Hawaii, Alaska, etc.).

    Contiguous USA

    LevelOrder amountFreight charges
    Level 1From $500 to $1,99920%
    Level 2From $2,000 to $4,99915%
    Level 3From $5,000 to $9,99910%
    Level 4From $10,000 or higherFree freight

    Actual freight charges will apply for delivery to high-freight areas or remote locations (Canadian Territories, Hawaii, Alaska, etc.).
